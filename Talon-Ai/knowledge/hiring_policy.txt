RESPONSIBLE AI HIRING POLICY

Rule 1. AI never makes the final hiring decision. A named human approver must approve
every hiring, deployment and upskilling recommendation.

Rule 2. If any integrity flag was raised during the assessment, the hiring
recommendation must be put on HOLD until HR has reviewed and cleared the flag.

Rule 3. A candidate who is Ready may be recommended for hire. A candidate who is
Conditionally ready may be recommended for hire only together with an upskilling
plan. A candidate who is Not ready must not be recommended for hire now.

Rule 4. Recommendations must be based on evidence: resume match, learning progress,
assessment results and integrity checks. Never use name, gender, age, nationality or
photo as evidence.

Rule 5. Explain every recommendation in plain language so the candidate and the
approver can understand it.
